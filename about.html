Our Story

Sanjeevane Yoga and Acupuncture Studio is a welcoming space dedicated to helping people discover practical ways to care for body and mind.

Sanjeevane was born on February 9, 2014, from a simple yet powerful belief: Health is true wealth. We believe that every individual holds infinite potential, and to truly enjoy this vast universe, a vibrant state of health is essential.

Our journey began with a mission to spread health awareness in a way that is accessible, practical, and affordable for everyone. Nature operates on simple laws, and we believe human health should too. By teaching simple daily practices and sharing easy-to-follow tips, we help people reconnect with the rhythms of nature.

Our commitment is to guide you toward a life where you are physically fit, mentally strong, and spiritually elevated.

  • Personalised yoga guidance
  • Traditional acupuncture sessions
  • Relaxation and mindfulness education
  • Friendly, client-focused care
